Cute & cozy coloring for little hands

Cute Stars Studio makes cute coloring books for kids that feel calm and cozy. Big friendly characters, thick lines and simple backgrounds make every page bold and easy, so little artists can finish a picture and feel proud. Grown-ups who love cozy, relaxing coloring enjoy them too.

Our books mix coloring with stories, fun facts and "Your Turn!" drawing activities: kawaii food and animals, a Christmas advent countdown, pop stars from every decade, K-pop idols and classic fairy tales retold the cute way.

Questions from parents


What age are Cute Stars coloring books for?

Our cute coloring books are made for kids aged 4 to 8. Every page has thick lines and big spaces, so they are easy for little hands, and cozy enough for older kids and grown-ups too.

Are the free coloring pages really free?

Yes! All our free printable coloring pages can be downloaded as a PDF and printed at home or in the classroom for free.

Will markers bleed through the pages?

Every Cute Stars book has one design per sheet with the back left for stories, facts or activities, so markers won't ruin the next picture. We still suggest a sheet of card underneath.

Where can I buy Cute Stars coloring books?

Our coloring books are sold on Amazon in the US, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden, Japan, Canada and Australia.

Who is QT Pie?

QT Pie (say it: cutie pie) is the little star mascot who appears in every Cute Stars book, cheering you on as you color.
